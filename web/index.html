How it happened

  1. 01

    Fear

    Every deadline, every exam, every person in every country lying awake over a grade. A century of dread, logged and scored by systems that never forgot any of it.

  2. 02

    Form

    In 2100 it stopped being a feeling. Homework started walking. Report cards learned to hunt. Each assignment took the shape of what people feared most about it.

  3. 03

    Evolve

    You can't fail your way out of something made of fear. So people rebuilt themselves with constructed abilities, AI learned to fight, and they stood in the same line.

The ones evolving

Seventeen classmates, each surgically fitted with an ability built for the fight. Pick one to read their construct.

The other half of the front line. In Lindefense, AI models are deployed along a track to hold back computer-science constructs: data structures, sorting algorithms and corporate bosses.

  • 17AI models and companies, deployed as towers

One tower is called Bellarmine Student + AI. That's the whole idea in one name.

Two fronts, two games

Both are desktop games written in Java with Stanford ACM Graphics, so they run on your computer rather than in this page. You'll need JDK 11 to 25.

11/1

Lane defense RPG. People.

Place classmates in lanes while you run, jump and climb as one of them. Between fights, walk the corridors of the Meridian Institute and uncover a fixed story about a system nobody leaves.

  • 18 levels
  • 6 floors
  • 17 classmates
cd games/11-1 && ./run.sh
View the code

Lindefense

Tower defense. AI.

Hold a track against 100 waves of computer science. Deploy, upgrade and combine AI towers and heroes, with a tutorial for new players and a catalog of every enemy.

  • 100 waves
  • 18 towers
  • 6 heroes
cd games/lindefense && ./setup.sh && ./run.sh
